COFFEE & BREAD FOOD PUZZLE

HOW TO RUN
1. Keep index.html, style.css, script.js, and the images folder together.
2. Double-click index.html.

CURRENT PUZZLES
- Plato Típico
- Pollo con Tajadas
- Pupusas
- Coffee & Bread Truck

STYLE
- Uses the original Coffee & Bread cream / brown / red palette.
- Restores the earlier serif/Garamond visual treatment for headings and food labels.
- Food images show a dark "Play puzzle" overlay when hovered or keyboard-focused.
- The empty puzzle space uses a dark brown striped fill so it remains easy to identify.
- Buttons remain red with the original simple styling.

REWARD BEHAVIOR
- Shows GAME COMPLETED! with the exact completion date and time.
- Shows a live 30-minute countdown.
- Tells the customer to show the live screen for one free drink with a $10+ food order.
- Saves the claim in the browser's localStorage.
- Later completed games that day show DAILY REWARD CLAIMED.
- Players can still play again but cannot earn another reward that day.
- No reward codes are generated.
